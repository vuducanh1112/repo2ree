Lifecycle

The REE from its creation to its last action, every operation in the order the journal recorded it, across every component, with the workbench sessions of its store and the API's journals beside them. Lines are placed in order, not to scale: a dashed rule marks where time passed between them, and says how much. Solid arrows are journal records the checker reads; dashed ones come from spans; dotted ones are matched by time only. The right column says where each line comes from. Reads, polling a run among them, are left out.
