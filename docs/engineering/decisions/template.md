# NNNN — Decision title

- **Status:** proposed
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Decision owners:** names or team

## Context

What forces a decision? State the constraints and the problem without assuming
the answer.

## Decision

State the choice and the boundary it establishes.

## Alternatives considered

List credible alternatives and why they were not selected. Do not add straw
alternatives merely to make the decision look inevitable.

## Consequences

Record both benefits and costs. Include operational, security, compatibility,
testing, and migration consequences where relevant.

## Revisit when

Name evidence or changed constraints that should cause a new decision. Omit
this section only when no concrete trigger is known.

## Evidence

Link to the living architecture, enforcement, implementation, tests, or prior
records that let a reader verify the decision's current application.
